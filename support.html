Support

Nocturne is a small, independently-built app. For anything — a bug, a crash, a feature request, a question about how something works, or a report about content shown in the app — reach out directly:

nocturne@vishvak.com

Common questions

Is Nocturne affiliated with Hacker News or Y Combinator?

No. Nocturne is an independent, unofficial reader for the public Hacker News API. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by Y Combinator.

Do I need an account to use Nocturne?

No — there are no accounts anywhere in the app. Browsing, saving stories, saving comments, and reading offline all work without signing in to anything.

What does the $1.99 unlock?

Nocturne Pro is a single, one-time purchase — no subscription. It unlocks the Ask HN / Show HN / Jobs feeds, saving individual comments, offline article reading, and two additional themes (AMOLED and a light Hacker News-styled theme). See the feature table on the home page for the full breakdown.

Can I get a refund?

All purchases go through Apple's App Store, so refunds are handled by Apple directly — see Apple's official refund process.

How do I save a comment, or block someone?

On a comments screen, swipe a comment right to save it (Pro), or swipe left (or long-press anywhere on it) for its actions: save, block, report to support, or view and flag it on Hacker News. Tap a name to fold that branch of the thread.

Where did the sort menu go?

Every feed is now a pill under “Hacker News” at the top of Home — swipe the row sideways to see them all. Ask HN, Show HN, and Jobs are marked PRO.

How do I see the welcome tour again?

Settings → About → Welcome tour. You can also switch haptics off under Settings → Appearance.

I found something objectionable in a comment.

Swipe left on (or long-press) any comment in the app for a "Report to support" action, or email the address above directly with a link to the comment. Nocturne also lets you block a specific user or turn on a local content filter from Settings.
